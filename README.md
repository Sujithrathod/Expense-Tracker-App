# Expense Tracker

**Your expenses are added automatically.** Whenever you pay with UPI, a debit card, a credit card
or at an ATM, your bank sends you a "debited" SMS. Expense Tracker reads that SMS, adds the expense
with the amount, the shop or person you paid and a category (Food, Travel, Shopping, Bills…), and
sends you a notification like **"Added ₹250 · Swiggy"**. You don't have to type anything.

Your messages are read only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

---

## How to install

### 1. Download the app
On your phone, open
**[Releases](https://github.com/Sujithrathod/Expense-Tracker-App/releases/latest)** and tap the
**`.apk`** file to download it.

### 2. Pause Play Protect
Android blocks apps that read SMS if they don't come from the Play Store, so pause Play Protect
for a moment:

1. Open the **Play Store**.
2. Tap your **profile picture** (top-right) → **Play Protect**.
3. Tap **⚙️** (top-right) → turn **off** **Scan apps with Play Protect**.

### 3. Install
Open the downloaded `.apk` file and tap **Install**.

### 4. Turn Play Protect back on
⚠️ **Don't forget this step.** Go back to **Play Store → profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙️** and
turn **Scan apps with Play Protect** back **on**.

### 5. Allow restricted settings
1. Long-press the **Expense Tracker** icon → **App info**
   (or **Settings → Apps → Expense Tracker**).
2. Tap **⋮** (3 dots, top-right) → **Allow restricted settings**.

### 6. Allow Notifications and SMS
Still in **App info**:

1. **Notifications** → turn **on**.
2. **Permissions** → **SMS** → **Allow**.

### 7. Turn on auto-add
Open **Expense Tracker** → tap **⚙️** → turn on **Auto-add from SMS**.

That's it. From now on, every payment SMS from your bank is added automatically and marked with
**💬 SMS**. Only messages that arrive after you turn it on are added.
